# BrandScope — Decision brief

## Business question
How can shopper insights guide profitable growth?

Market: All markets. Analysis window: full. Source: UCI Online Retail, 2010–2011; GBP. Historical gift retailer with wholesale buyers, not P&G data or Swedish FMCG market data.

## Observed evidence
- Net merchandise sales: £9,754,656
- Identified shoppers: 4,334
- Repeat shopper rate: 65.3%

## Proposed campaign: Win back valuable shoppers
All inputs below are assumptions. No campaign was run.
- Eligible rollout audience: 2000
- Baseline conversion: 5%
- Assumed incremental conversion: 3 percentage points
- Order value: GBP 45; margin: 45%; discount on all treatment purchases: 5%
- Additional campaign cost: GBP 600
- Modelled incremental contribution: £255
- Approximate conversion sample per arm: 1059; enough audience: false

## Experiment and decision
Randomize eligible consented shoppers 50:50 into treatment and no-message control. Fixed 30-day outcome window. Primary outcome: contribution per eligible shopper. Secondary: purchase conversion. Power monetary outcome from pilot variance before launch; the sample calculator powers conversion only. Guardrails: returns, opt-outs, complaints. Scale only with positive contribution supported by uncertainty and acceptable guardrails.

## Communication concept
EN: A thoughtful follow-up, based on what you liked.
SV draft: En omtänksam uppföljning, baserad på det du gillade.
Swedish copy requires local review.

## Limitations
No observed margins, channel exposure, campaign response, market share or brand awareness. RFM ranking diagnostics are not causal lift. Synthetic event feed is isolated from the historical data.
