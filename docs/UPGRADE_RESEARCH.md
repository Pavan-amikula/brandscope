# Research behind the AI/ML upgrade

Reviewed public sources on 25 October 2026. LinkedIn public pages were read without signing in, messaging anyone or scraping account data. Social posts inform project positioning; statistical choices are grounded in primary technical documentation. Self-reported results in other portfolios are not treated as verified benchmarks or copied into this project.

## Scope decisions

The upgrade implements supervised purchase propensity, learned product factors, TF-IDF evidence retrieval and fixed-horizon experiment analysis. It does not add deep learning simply for a keyword, invent a generative model integration, fabricate field experiments, or rename gift-retail data as P&G consumer-goods data.

The new algorithms are useful because they make specific decisions inspectable: who is likely to purchase, what product hypotheses are supported, what evidence answers a stakeholder question, and whether a randomized test supports a contribution win. More algorithms alone do not establish brand management competence; the marketing interpretation and missing consumer evidence remain central.
